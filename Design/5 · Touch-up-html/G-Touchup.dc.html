How to use
  1. Pick the red brush and paint over what should go (e.g. a reflection).
  2. Pick the green brush and paint over parts of the product that are missing.
  3. Click Apply. The cut-out is updated and the final image is re-made (about 2 s).
Use the editor's eraser or undo to fix a stroke before applying. Zoom with the mouse wheel for small details.
